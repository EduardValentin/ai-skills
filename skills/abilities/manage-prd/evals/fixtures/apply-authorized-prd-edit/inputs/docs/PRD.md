# Learning Plans

## Problem

Coaches need a reliable way to assign and revisit learning plans with clients.

## Goals

- Let coaches guide each client through an assigned learning plan.
- Keep plan status understandable to coaches and clients.

## Roles and permissions

- Coaches can create and assign plans to their clients.
- Clients can view active plans assigned to them.

## Plan lifecycle

- Active plans are visible to the assigning coach and client.
- Archived plans remain available in plan history.

## Notifications

- Clients are notified when a coach assigns a plan.

## Non-goals

- Automatically recommending learning content.
- Defining coach billing or subscription rules.
